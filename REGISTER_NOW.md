### Step 2: Register Your Account

1. Go to your website: **http://localhost:3002**

2. Click **"Create Account"** or go to Register page

3. Fill in the form:
   ```
   Full Name: Your Name
   Email: your.email@example.com
   Password: (your password)
   Account Type: Training or OJT
   (fill other fields as needed)
   ```

4. Click **"Register"**

5. ✅ Should see: **"🎉 Registration successful! You can now login."**

### Step 3: Login

1. Go to Login page

2. Select **account type** (Trainee or OJT Student)

4. Click **"Login"**

5. ✅ Should see: **"✅ Login successful! Welcome [Your Name]"**

6. 🎉 You'll be redirected to your dashboard!
